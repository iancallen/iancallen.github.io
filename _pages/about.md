Hello! I am a Ph.D. student in Education Policy at the [University of Virginia](https://education.virginia.edu/). Before starting my doctorate, I was an Instructor of Economics in the Department of Agricultural Economics and Economics at [Montana State University](https://www.montana.edu/econ/) and a Research Associate at the Center for Analysis of Longitudinal Data in Education Research [(CALDER)](https://caldercenter.org/) at the American Institutes for Research. I hold an M.S. in Applied Economics from [Montana State University](https://www.montana.edu/), and a B.A. in Economics and a B.S. in Statistics from [Western Washington University](https://www.wwu.edu/).
